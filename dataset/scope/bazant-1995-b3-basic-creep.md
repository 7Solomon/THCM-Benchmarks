# bazant-1995-b3-basic-creep

**Domain:** viscoelasticity (basic creep), 2D, sealed cylinder under sustained compression
**Fields:** displacement
**Creep:** B3 basic creep, loaded at t' = 28 d at sigma = 0.4 fc
**Damage:** none
**Output:** Axial strain history over the load duration, against the B3 compliance function (Eq. 1.7 with approximate Q, Eq. 1.35) -- worked example Sec. 1.4.5.
