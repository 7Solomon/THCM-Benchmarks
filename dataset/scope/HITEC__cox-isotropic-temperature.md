# HITEC/cox-isotropic-temperature

**Domain:** thermal, heat conduction only, 2D axisymmetric-style near field, 10 a
**Fields:** temperature
**Creep:** none
**Damage:** none
**Output:** Temperature histories at observation points P1 (r=1.25 m), P2 (1.90 m), P3 (2.50 m), P4 (6.25 m) over 10 years at 200 W/m (Fig. 7), overlaid on all seven participating codes.
