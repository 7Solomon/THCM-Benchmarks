# ATENA/TPBB

**Domain:** mechanical, 2D, quasi-static (pseudo-time)
**Fields:** displacement
**Creep:** none
**Damage:** Rankine damage, smeared crack (rotating and fixed variants)
**Output:** Load vs mid-span deflection of the three-point bending beam (Fig. 1-2). Peak load gives the flexural strength, compared against CEB-FIP MC90 Eq. 1.1.
