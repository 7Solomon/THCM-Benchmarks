# bazant-2004-fahmi-transitional-thermal-creep

**Domain:** thermo-mechanical, 2D, sealed specimen under sustained load with temperature steps
**Fields:** temperature, displacement
**Creep:** B3 with transitional thermal creep (thermal ageing)
**Damage:** none (linear elastic)
**Output:** Axial strain vs time over 155 d against the digitized experimental curve (Fahmi et al. 1972, Fig. 5), including the unloading branch; plus the imposed temperature history.
