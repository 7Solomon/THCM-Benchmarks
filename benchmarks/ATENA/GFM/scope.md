# ATENA/GFM

**Domain:** mechanical, 2D, quasi-static (pseudo-time)
**Fields:** displacement
**Creep:** none
**Damage:** rotating smeared crack, fracture-energy softening
**Output:** Load vs load-point opening for the double cantilever beam, short notch a1=100 mm (Fig. 1-4). Fracture energy Gf back-computed from the curve; reference Gf = 122 N/m.
