# faria-2006-slab/thermo_mechanical

**Domain:** thermo-chemo-mechanical, 2D fully restrained through-thickness strip, 0.35 m slab
**Fields:** temperature, degree of hydration, displacement
**Creep:** double power law (ageing viscoelastic)
**Damage:** none (ageing linear elastic)
**Output:** Longitudinal stress sigma_x at the centre vs time, with creep (Fig. 9a); eps_x at 5 cm from the bottom (Fig. 18); the infinite-slab stress bound for the Fig. 17 P3 overlay. Thermal side: Fig. 7a/7b and Fig. 8b.
